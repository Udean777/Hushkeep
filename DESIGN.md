# Hushkeep visual direction

Hushkeep should feel quiet, private, and warm. It is a personal memory vault, not a social feed or productivity dashboard.

- Palette: warm paper and ink as the foundation, deep moss for primary actions, and clay for meaningful attention states. The colors are moderately saturated and intentionally avoid pastel surfaces.
- Typography: platform sans-serif for utility and a serif date role for the editorial archive voice.
- Composition: one clear focal action per screen, date-led grouping, a featured memory, and varied photo tiles instead of a generic dashboard grid.
- Motion: shared photo transitions, upload state feedback, and short state-change animations. No infinite decorative motion.
- Motif: the memory ribbon, a small moss rule with a clay point, and date-led grouping echo the idea of memories being carefully kept.
- Dial: ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 3 / MOTION 2.

Decision reasons:

- Warm neutrals reduce the feeling of an exposed social product and keep photos central.
- Moss is reserved for save, sync, and primary actions so status remains legible.
- Date-led grouping serves the timeline model instead of imitating a feed.
- Limited motion prevents a private archive from feeling gamified.
- The paper, moss, and clay roles keep the interface bright enough to feel alive without becoming pastel or neon.
- The dark theme uses a charcoal-green ground so photographs remain the focal point without forcing a pure black UI.
- Featured memory plus compact tiles gives the timeline a visual rhythm while keeping the content model date-led.
